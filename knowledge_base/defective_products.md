# Defective Product Policy

1. A functional defect reported within 14 calendar days of delivery is eligible for replacement.
2. For orders valued above ₹3,000, basic evidence of the defect must be requested before replacement is approved.
3. Defects reported more than 14 days after delivery are not eligible under the standard defect policy.
4. Cosmetic damage should be evaluated under the Damaged Goods Policy.
5. If the nature of the defect or delivery date is missing, request more information.
