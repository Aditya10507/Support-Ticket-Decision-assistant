# Wrong Item Policy

1. A wrong item or wrong flavour must be reported within 7 calendar days of delivery.
2. Eligible reports should receive a replacement of the correct item.
3. If the originally ordered item is unavailable, offer a refund.
4. Reports submitted more than 7 days after delivery are not eligible under the standard wrong-item policy.
5. If the report does not identify what was ordered versus what was received, request more information.
