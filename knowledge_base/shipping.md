# Shipping and Delivery Policy

1. Standard domestic orders are expected to arrive within 5 calendar days after dispatch.
2. If an order has not arrived 6 or 7 days after dispatch, advise the customer to wait and continue tracking the shipment.
3. If an order has not arrived 8 to 10 days after dispatch, open a shipping investigation.
4. If an order has not arrived more than 10 days after dispatch, offer a replacement or refund.
5. If dispatch date or delivery status is unclear, request more information.
